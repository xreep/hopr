# Routing-engine benchmark

Generated by `npm run benchmark --workspace=backend` on 2026-10-22. Instances are reproducible (seeded): stops spread within about 8 km of the five demo hubs (New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Ranchi), 40% with a delivery window of 60–105 minutes, 15% urgent, departing 10:00 IST on a weekday. Costs use the planner's own model (driver time, fuel, late-window penalties, priority), as in section 4.2 of the technical documentation.

- **Hopr** is the search the planner uses: exact up to 8 stops, then local search with 2-opt, Or-opt chains and swaps, plus iterated local search.
- **Previous** is the search used before: two starting orders improved with 2-opt and single-stop moves.
- **Nearest neighbour** always drives to the closest remaining stop, which is roughly what planning by eye gives.
